- R2: Indigenous land/treaty dispute **1976/1982**, High Court speech **25 March 2013**; R14: Joh’s negotiated Torres Strait marine-park/treaty proposal **2 April 1974**, Hansard p3417.
- R4/R6: emergency/protest policing **1971–1979**, SLQ **2019** and Brennan **2007**. These support security preference, not a literal digital-monitoring policy.

His practical record does not always honour that rhetoric; the statement still prevents equating public faith with exclusive confessional government or rejection of every civil conscience.
