# Question-wording audit — issue #7

Source: [Improve question wording](https://github.com/RomanCypherpunk/12axes/issues/7).
Baseline: [`a1dc5b4`](https://github.com/RomanCypherpunk/12axes/commit/a1dc5b448d85845d62b7e99509dc007255b16bdb), “feat(quiz): review some questions”. That fix changed 131 Portuguese and 147 English texts and correctly made `representacao_12` a conditional preference for authoritarian government.

This report describes the **current proposed wording**, including the full-pool second review requested in the [owner's PR comment](https://github.com/RomanCypherpunk/12axes/pull/21#issuecomment-5908381671). The original 76-item proposal is preserved in commit `c781840`; its superseded rationales are replaced here rather than presented as current decisions.

## Scope and decision rule

Reviewed all 240 main-pool items in Portuguese and English, including all 76 originally changed items and each item's neighbors on the same axis. Each item must ask for support for one position, rather than agreement that a policy can work or that an outcome can occur. Explicit ethical judgements and conditional tradeoffs are valid preferences. Remove leading benefits, identify the policy being supported, and distinguish overlapping items by institution, decision, scope, or cost accepted.

Preserve each item's original subject where possible. Bundles are narrowed to one proposition without transplanting unrelated subjects into neighboring slots. The rationales identify retired subtopics and substantial changes of scope. Related questions can deliberately test different applications or degrees of a position; distinctness does not mean every item measures an unrelated concept. In particular, a general principle and a specific exception, or support with different costs, are not automatically duplicates. The review ledger records the actual target of every item so these decisions can be checked.

Portuguese retains Brazilian references where appropriate; English uses global equivalents without changing the decision. Both versions share IDs, order, agreement poles and weights. The Portuguese audit template exactly matches the live pool. The 240-question structure and ten questions per pole per axis are unchanged.

Election and archetype questionnaires are separate instruments and outside this main-pool review. Existing saved results, profile vectors and historical profile answers are not recomputed. Unchanged scoring mechanics do not establish equivalence of responses to reworded items. Fresh profile audits must use the updated template; profile recalibration remains separate work.
