| `estrutura` | In **1972–1975**, he resisted Commonwealth control and defended state railways as a development instrument; he also resisted ceding Torres Strait islands. This supports federal autonomy. In **1987**, his government overrode local planning decisions: state autonomy is not general local or Indigenous autonomy. [Megarrity, ANU Press, 2022](https://press-files.anu.edu.au/downloads/press/n10204/html/02_megarrity.xhtml?page=6&referer=), [ABC, 1 January 2018](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2018-01-01/sir-joh-bjelke-petersen-qld-30-year-cabinet-documents-released/9270744). | High for states; qualified for municipalities and secession. |
| `representacao` | He retained parliamentary elections, while the **1985 Electoral Districts Bill** retained unequal regional electoral arrangements. Cabinet records describe concentrated executive decision-making through **1987**. Electorally sustained personal power does not imply abolishing elections or universal adult suffrage. [Queensland Hansard, 27 March 1985](https://documents.parliament.qld.gov.au/events/han/1985/1985_03_27.pdf), [Queensland cabinet release, 1 January 2018](https://statements.qld.gov.au/statements/83428). | High for the institutional distinction; referendums and recall are inferred. |
| `poder` | The **1971 Springbok emergency** and restrictions on political processions culminated in the **1977** protest conflict. In **1985**, government intervention in SEQEB dismissed 1,002 striking electricians and escalated to emergency powers. These support coercive public-order preferences, separately from economic ownership. [SLQ, 2019, Remembering the Revolution](https://www.slq.qld.gov.au/blog/remembering-revolution), [SLQ, 17 March 2017, SEQEB](https://www.slq.qld.gov.au/blog/la-fetts-seqeb-scabs). | High for protest and industrial coercion; digital surveillance, vaccination and firearm analogies are inferred. |
| `imigracao` | Resistance to the multicultural turn of the **1970s** is documented by Jupp. However, the **1979** state cabinet approved a limited multicultural curriculum that separated multicultural education from bilingual and language programs. This qualifies the assimilation judgment and does not justify total immigration closure. [Jupp, ANU Press, July 2011](https://press-files.anu.edu.au/downloads/press/p113381/html/ch02.xhtml?page=6&referer=310), [Michael Singh in Language Australia, 2001, pp.123–148](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED471603.pdf). | Medium; immigration admission was primarily federal, and some cultural preferences are inferred. |
| `diplomacia` | In **1970**, he denounced a draft-resistance centre using treason language, supporting an inference of defence and allied-war support. Yet the **August 1987** Romanian commercial negotiations show willingness to negotiate with an ideological adversary. This does not establish an aggressive war or autonomous-weapons doctrine. [Queensland Journal of Labour History, March 2011](https://www.labourhistory.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2012/09/QJLH-No-12-March-2011-final.pdf), [Queensland cabinet release, 1 January 2018](https://statements.qld.gov.au/statements/83428). | Medium-low; most military questions extrapolate from a state premier's conduct. |
| `intervencao` | His **1970s** Torres Strait boundary campaign resisted external concessions and prioritised Queensland sovereignty. His **1987** Romanian trip promoted coal sales rather than military intervention. This supports assertive diplomatic interests, with limited evidence about overseas bases, sanctions or humanitarian deployments. [ABC, 15 August 2013](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2013-08-15/wordsworth-patrolling-the-short-hop-from-png-to-australia/4886290), [ABC, 1 January 2018](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2018-01-01/sir-joh-bjelke-petersen-qld-30-year-cabinet-documents-released/9270744). | Medium-low; sovereignty is documented, deployment doctrine is inferred. |
| `economia` | He welcomed overseas mining capital in the **1970s**, defended state-owned railways, and personally defended Queensland's **free public hospitals on 7 February 1984**. His **1985** union confrontation favoured employers. The resulting mixed economy is historically more specific than blanket privatisation or collective ownership. [Megarrity, ANU Press, 2022](https://press-files.anu.edu.au/downloads/press/n10204/html/02_megarrity.xhtml?page=6&referer=), [Queensland Hansard, 7 February 1984, p.1375](https://documents.parliament.qld.gov.au/events/han/1984/1984_02_07.pdf), [SLQ, 17 March 2017](https://www.slq.qld.gov.au/blog/la-fetts-seqeb-scabs). | High for named industries and hospitals; welfare and prison analogies are inferred. |
| `controle` | The **1968 Goonyella agreement** and subsequent statutory development arrangements combined private enterprise with state facilitation. **1986 amendments to the Regulation of Sugar Prices Act** aided adjustment after falling world prices. Preferential project approvals in **1987** also show intervention rather than universal competitive neutrality. [Wanna and Arklay, ANU Press, 2010, chapter 12](https://press-files.anu.edu.au/downloads/press/p69721/html/ch12.xhtml?page=16&referer=), [ABC, 1 January 2018](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2018-01-01/sir-joh-bjelke-petersen-qld-30-year-cabinet-documents-released/9270744). | High for selective development direction; central banking and cryptocurrency are inferred. |
| `comercio` | Foreign investment, mineral export agreements in **1968** and protection of the sugar industry in **1986** distinguish export-oriented development from both autarky and unqualified free trade. The **1987** Romanian coal proposal further demonstrates trading across ideological divisions. [Wanna and Arklay, ANU Press, 2010, chapter 12](https://press-files.anu.edu.au/downloads/press/p69721/html/ch12.xhtml?page=16&referer=), [Queensland cabinet release, 1 January 2018](https://statements.qld.gov.au/statements/83428). | High for exports and protected agriculture; modern tariff examples are analogies. |
| `religiao` | His **1968–1987** public career was informed by Lutheran faith. The **10 May 2005** parliamentary condolence explicitly connects his Christian commitments with his practical values; the **3 May 2005** funeral coverage corroborates the public religious identity. `religions: ["christianity"]` follows this evidence and the computed religious-axis requirement; no `only` marker is used. [Queensland Hansard, 10 May 2005, p.1164](https://documents.parliament.qld.gov.au/events/han/2005/2005_05_10_WEEKLY.pdf), [ABC, 3 May 2005](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2005-05-03/kingaroy-stops-for-joh-funeral/1562754). | High; official state-religion and church-tax questions are inferred, without assuming theocracy. |

The censorship and sexual conservatism of his premiership are separately documented. Gender, adoption and poliamory answers are projections from this record. Meat-consumption reduction receives `D`, not categorical `DT`: a cattle farmer's livelihood establishes a preference, not hostility to every reduction. [SLQ, 2011, 1980 cabinet minutes](https://www.slq.qld.gov.au/blog/release-1980-queensland-government-cabinet-minutes), [Wanna and Arklay, ANU Press, 2010, chapter 8](https://press-files.anu.edu.au/downloads/press/p69721/html/ch08.xhtml?page=12&referer=). | High for abortion and censorship; modern cultural examples are inferred. |
| `tecnologia` | The **1968** coal-development agreements and infrastructure program favoured industrial growth. His farming career and continuing rural life qualify this enthusiasm. These facts support practical development and mechanisation, while moral limits on human enhancement remain an inference rather than an attributed AI or transhumanist platform. [Wanna and Arklay, ANU Press, 2010, chapter 12](https://press-files.anu.edu.au/downloads/press/p69721/html/ch12.xhtml?page=16&referer=), [Queensland Parliament, 10 May 2005](https://documents.parliament.qld.gov.au/events/han/2005/2005_05_10_WEEKLY.pdf). | Medium for industry; low for technologies developed after his premiership. |

[High Court Chief Justice Robert French, 25 March 2013](https://www.hcourt.gov.au/assets/publications/speeches/current-justices/frenchcj/frenchcj25mar13.pdf).
