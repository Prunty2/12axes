# Pauline Hanson: new personality audit

Proposal: [issue #48](https://github.com/RomanCypherpunk/12axes/issues/48). Research cutoff: **2 October 2026**. Catalog: `personality`; ID: `pauline-hanson`; category: `politico`; lifespan: `1954–`.

This addition supplies an Australian reference for the combination of assimilationist nationalism, protected domestic industry, citizen referendums, constitutional federalism and strategic public services. It was assessed separately from the other proposed Australian personalities. No answers from previous pull requests or neighboring personalities were consulted. All 240 question answers and five archetype choices are preserved in `profile-audit/answers/personality/pauline-hanson.json`.

## Research and method

The research covered each axis before drafting the metadata. Independent publishers include Parliament of Australia, ABC, SBS and Wikimedia, alongside first-party policy statements. Publication dates are distinguished from the access date; undated policy pages below were checked on 2 October 2026. The latest migration proposal supersedes the party's earlier migration formulations.

The audit interpreted Brazil-specific question examples as analogous Australian policy choices. Each answer is a simulation grounded in the evidence, rather than a claim that Hanson personally answered that exact question. The briefs preserve the distinction between federalism and democracy, between military deterrence and foreign intervention, and between Christian cultural advocacy and personal religious observance. The current archetype block was generated with `profile_vector.py --prompt-block`; the final vector was computed with `profile_vector.compute_vector`, including the archetype effects. It was never placed on the axes by hand.
